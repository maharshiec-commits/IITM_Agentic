1. FIXED DEPOSIT (TERM DEPOSIT) INTEREST RATE MATRIX

- 7 days to 45 days: 3.50%
- 46 days to 179 days: 4.75%

2. RETAIL LOAN PRODUCTS
2.1 Home Loans (Repo-Rate Linked Benchmark - EBLR: 6.50% + Spread)
- Interest Rate: 8.40% to 9.25% p.a. (Floating, linked to CIBIL score)
  * CIBIL >= 800: 8.40% p.a.
  * CIBIL 750 - 799: 8.65% p.a.
  * CIBIL 700 - 749: 9.00% p.a.
- Loan-to-Value (LTV) Ratio:
  * Loans up to INR 30 Lakhs: Up to 90% of property cost
  * Loans INR 30 Lakhs to 75 Lakhs: Up to 80% of property cost
  * Loans above INR 75 Lakhs: Up to 75% of property cost
- Maximum Tenure: 30 years (or age 70 at retirement, whichever is earlier)
- Pre-payment / Foreclosure penalty: NIL for floating rate home loans per RBI regulations

2.2 Personal Loans (Unsecured)
- Interest Rate: 10.50% to 15.00% p.a. (Fixed, based on corporate salary segment and credit score)
- Loan Quantum: INR 50,000 to INR 25,00,000
- Repayment Tenure: 12 months to 60 months (1 to 5 years)
- Processing Fee: 1.50% of loan amount (Min INR 1,500, Max INR 10,000) + GST
- Pre-closure Charges: 3% of outstanding principal if closed within 12-24 months; 1.5% after 24 months; Nil after 36 months

2.3 Auto / Vehicle Loans (New Cars)
- Interest Rate: 8.75% to 9.75% p.a.
- Financing: Up to 90% on on-road price
- Tenure: 12 to 84 months (1 to 7 years)
- Processing fee: INR 2,500 flat + GST

3. LOAN EMI COMPUTATION FORMULA & PRINCIPLES
- Standard Reducing Balance EMI calculation:
  EMI = [P x r x (1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n - 1]
  where P = Principal loan amount, r = Monthly interest rate (Annual rate / 12 / 100), n = Loan tenure in months.
- Pre-approved loan offers: Subject to final bureau validation and income verification. Agent must never guarantee credit approval.
