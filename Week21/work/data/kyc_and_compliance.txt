APEX GLOBAL BANK — KYC (KNOW YOUR CUSTOMER) & COMPLIANCE GUIDELINES
Document ID: AGB-KYC-2026-V2
Regulatory Authority: Reserve Bank of India Master Direction on KYC

1. OFFICIALLY VALID DOCUMENTS (OVD) FOR IDENTITY & ADDRESS
1.1 Individual Customers
Customers must provide at least one Officially Valid Document (OVD) containing identity and address:
- Passport (Mandatory for Non-Resident accounts)
- Driving Licence (Must be valid and unexpired)
- Proof of possession of Aadhaar number (Offline XML verification or masked e-Aadhaar where first 8 digits are redacted)
- Voter's Identity Card issued by Election Commission of India
- Job card issued by NREGA duly signed by an officer of the State Government
- Letter issued by the National Population Register containing details of name and address

1.2 PAN Card Requirement
- Permanent Account Number (PAN) or Form 60 (for non-taxable income) is mandatory for:
  * Opening any bank account
  * Cash deposits exceeding INR 50,000 in a single day
  * Purchase of foreign currency exceeding INR 50,000
  * Opening Fixed Deposits exceeding INR 50,000

2. PERIODIC KYC RE-AUTHENTICATION (RE-KYC)
Under RBI guidelines, existing customers must undergo periodic KYC updation based on their assigned risk rating:
- High Risk Customers: Re-KYC required every 2 years
- Medium Risk Customers: Re-KYC required every 8 years
- Low Risk Customers: Re-KYC required every 10 years

2.1 Re-KYC Submission Channels
- Video KYC (V-KYC): Available via mobile banking app between 8 AM - 8 PM on working days
- Self-Declaration: For Low Risk customers with no change in address, a simple digital declaration via Net Banking suffices
- Branch Submission: Hard copies of self-attested OVDs submitted at any designated home or host branch

3. ADDRESS CHANGE PROTOCOL
3.1 Online Mode (Aadhaar OTP-based)
- If the new address matches the address on Aadhaar, customer can complete address modification in 5 minutes via Net Banking using Aadhaar OTP authentication.
3.2 Offline / Discrepant Address Mode
- If current residential address differs from Aadhaar address, customer must submit a secondary address proof (Utility bill not older than 2 months, Rent Agreement registered with sub-registrar, or employer letter).
- Physical address verification letter dispatched by speed post with verification code.

4. NON-RESIDENT ACCOUNTS (NRE / NRO)
4.1 Non-Resident External (NRE) Account
- Maintained in Indian Rupees; funds repatriable outside India without ceiling
- Interest earned is 100% tax-free in India
- Credits permitted only from foreign inward remittances

4.2 Non-Resident Ordinary (NRO) Account
- Maintained in Indian Rupees for managing legitimate Indian income (rent, dividend, pension)
- Repatriation permitted up to USD 1,00,000 per financial year subject to Form 15CA/CB documentation
- Interest earned is subject to Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) at 30% + applicable surcharge

5. ANTI-MONEY LAUNDERING (AML) & SUSPICIOUS TRANSACTION REPORTING (STR)
- Cash transactions exceeding INR 10,00,000 in a month trigger automatic Cash Transaction Reports (CTR) to Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-IND)
- Rapid structuring of deposits beneath INR 50,000 to circumvent PAN requirements is automatically flagged as potential smurfing
- Accounts flagged for AML investigations undergo restricted debit status pending compliance clearance within 48 hours
