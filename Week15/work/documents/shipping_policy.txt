ShopEase E-Commerce - Shipping & Delivery Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Document ID: POL-SHP-001

1. SHIPPING OPTIONS

1.1 Standard Shipping
- Delivery Time: 5-7 business days
- Cost: Free for orders above INR 499; INR 49 for orders below INR 499
- Available for all serviceable pin codes across India
- Tracking information provided via email and SMS

1.2 Express Shipping
- Delivery Time: 2-3 business days
- Cost: INR 99 for orders above INR 499; INR 149 for orders below INR 499
- Available in metro cities and select Tier-2 cities
- Real-time tracking available

1.3 Same-Day Delivery
- Delivery Time: Same day (orders placed before 12:00 PM)
- Cost: INR 199
- Available only in: Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune
- Not available for items shipped from warehouse (only local sellers)
- Orders placed after 12:00 PM will be delivered next business day

1.4 Scheduled Delivery
- Choose your preferred delivery date and 4-hour time slot
- Available for orders with delivery address in metro cities
- Cost: INR 79 on top of standard shipping
- Must be scheduled at least 2 days in advance

2. SHIPPING RESTRICTIONS

2.1 Weight & Size Limits
- Standard shipping: items up to 30 kg
- Items over 30 kg require special freight shipping (additional charges apply)
- Oversized items (longest dimension > 150 cm): INR 299 surcharge

2.2 Restricted Areas
- Certain remote areas may have extended delivery times (10-15 business days)
- Some products may not be shippable to certain regions due to local regulations
- International shipping is currently NOT available

2.3 Hazardous Materials
- Items classified as hazardous cannot be shipped via air transport
- Ground shipping only; delivery times may be extended
- Additional packaging requirements apply

3. ORDER PROCESSING

3.1 Processing Time
- Orders are typically processed within 1-2 business days
- Business days are Monday through Saturday (excluding national holidays)
- Orders placed after 5:00 PM are processed the next business day

3.2 Order Confirmation
- Order confirmation email sent immediately after purchase
- Shipping confirmation with tracking number sent when item ships
- Estimated delivery date provided at checkout and in confirmation email

3.3 Order Modifications
- Orders can be modified or cancelled within 1 hour of placement
- After 1 hour, orders that have entered processing cannot be modified
- Address changes may be possible if the item has not yet shipped (contact support)

4. TRACKING YOUR ORDER

4.1 How to Track
- Log into your ShopEase account > My Orders > Track Order
- Use the tracking number from your shipping confirmation email
- SMS tracking updates sent automatically at key milestones
- Track via WhatsApp: Send tracking number to +91-98765-00000

4.2 Tracking Statuses
- Order Placed: Order received and confirmed
- Processing: Order being prepared for shipment
- Shipped: Package picked up by courier
- In Transit: Package on the way to your city
- Out for Delivery: Package with delivery agent for final delivery
- Delivered: Package delivered successfully
- Failed Delivery Attempt: Delivery attempted but unsuccessful

5. DELIVERY POLICIES

5.1 Delivery Attempts
- A maximum of 3 delivery attempts will be made
- After 3 failed attempts, the package is returned to the warehouse
- A full refund (minus shipping charges) will be initiated for returned packages

5.2 Delivery Verification
- OTP verification required for orders above INR 5,000 or high-value electronics
- Photo proof of delivery captured for all orders
- Signature required for items above INR 10,000

5.3 Safe Drop
- For orders under INR 5,000, the delivery agent may leave the package at a safe location if the recipient is unavailable
- Safe drop location preference can be set in account settings
- Photo proof of safe drop will be sent via SMS/email

6. DAMAGED OR MISSING SHIPMENTS

6.1 Damaged in Transit
- If your package arrives damaged, report within 48 hours of delivery
- Take photos of the damaged package and item
- Contact support with your order number and photos
- Replacement or refund will be processed within 2 business days

6.2 Missing or Stolen Packages
- If tracking shows delivered but you haven't received it:
  1. Check with neighbors and building security
  2. Wait 24 hours (sometimes tracking updates before actual delivery)
  3. Contact support if still not found
- Investigation will be initiated and resolved within 5-7 business days

6.3 Incorrect Items
- If you receive the wrong item, contact support within 48 hours
- A prepaid return label will be provided at no cost
- The correct item will be shipped via express delivery at no additional charge
