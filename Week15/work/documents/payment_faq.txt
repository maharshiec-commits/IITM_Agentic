ShopEase E-Commerce - Payment Methods & FAQs
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Document ID: POL-PAY-001

1. ACCEPTED PAYMENT METHODS

1.1 Credit & Debit Cards
- Visa, Mastercard, American Express, RuPay
- Both domestic and international cards accepted
- 3D Secure (OTP) verification required for all card transactions
- Card details can be securely saved for faster checkout (tokenized, PCI-DSS compliant)

1.2 UPI (Unified Payments Interface)
- Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, and all UPI-enabled apps
- UPI ID payment supported
- QR code payment available at checkout
- Transaction limit: as per your bank's UPI limit (typically INR 1,00,000)

1.3 Net Banking
- Supported for all major Indian banks (50+ banks)
- Redirects to your bank's secure portal for authentication
- No additional charges for net banking payments

1.4 Digital Wallets
- ShopEase Wallet (loaded via UPI/Net Banking/Cards)
- Paytm Wallet, PhonePe Wallet
- Amazon Pay balance NOT accepted
- Wallet payments are instant with no additional verification

1.5 Cash on Delivery (COD)
- Available for orders up to INR 50,000
- COD handling fee: INR 29 per order
- Not available for: electronics above INR 25,000, international sellers, digital products
- Exact change preferred; delivery agents may carry limited change
- COD orders require phone verification before dispatch

1.6 EMI (Equated Monthly Installments)
- Available on credit cards from select banks
- No-Cost EMI available on select products (3, 6, 9, or 12 months)
- Standard EMI: interest rates as per card issuer (typically 12-18% per annum)
- Minimum order value for EMI: INR 3,000
- Bajaj Finserv EMI card also accepted

1.7 Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL)
- ShopEase PayLater: Pay within 30 days, no interest
- Simpl: Split into 3 payments over 3 months
- ZestMoney: EMI plans from 3-24 months
- Credit limit based on creditworthiness assessment

2. PAYMENT SECURITY

2.1 Security Measures
- All transactions are SSL encrypted (256-bit)
- PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment processing
- Tokenized card storage (actual card numbers never stored)
- Two-factor authentication for all transactions
- Fraud detection system monitors all transactions in real-time

2.2 Safe Shopping Guarantee
- ShopEase guarantees 100% protection against unauthorized transactions
- If you notice any unauthorized charge, report within 72 hours
- Full refund for verified unauthorized transactions within 5 business days

3. COMMON PAYMENT ISSUES

3.1 Payment Failed but Amount Debited
- If payment fails but your account is debited, the amount is automatically reversed
- Bank reversal timeline: 5-7 business days
- If not reversed within 7 days, contact support with transaction reference number

3.2 Double Payment Charged
- In rare cases, a transaction may be charged twice
- Contact support immediately with both transaction IDs
- Duplicate charge will be refunded within 3-5 business days

3.3 EMI Not Reflecting
- EMI conversion may take 3-5 business days to reflect on your card statement
- If EMI is not showing after 5 days, contact your card-issuing bank
- ShopEase support can provide EMI confirmation letter if needed

3.4 COD Order Verification
- COD orders require OTP verification via SMS before dispatch
- If OTP is not received, check registered mobile number in account settings
- COD is auto-cancelled if not verified within 24 hours

4. SHOPEASE WALLET

4.1 Features
- Load money via UPI, Net Banking, or Cards
- Instant checkout with no additional authentication
- Cashback rewards credited directly to wallet
- Minimum load: INR 100; Maximum balance: INR 1,00,000

4.2 Wallet Refunds
- Refunds to ShopEase Wallet are processed instantly
- Wallet balance does not expire
- Wallet balance can be withdrawn to bank account (processing: 3-5 business days)

5. INVOICE & GST

5.1 Invoice Generation
- Digital invoice generated for every order
- Available in My Orders > Order Details > Download Invoice
- Invoice sent to registered email automatically

5.2 GST Input Credit
- GST invoice available for business purchases
- Add your GSTIN in account settings for GST-compliant invoices
- GST rate applicable as per product category (5%, 12%, 18%, or 28%)
