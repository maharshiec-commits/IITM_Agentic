ShopEase E-Commerce - ShopEase Prime Membership Program
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Document ID: POL-MEM-001

1. PROGRAM OVERVIEW

ShopEase Prime is our premium membership program designed to provide exclusive benefits, faster shipping, and additional savings to our most valued customers.

1.1 Membership Tiers
- Prime Monthly: INR 149/month
- Prime Annual: INR 999/year (save INR 789 compared to monthly)
- Prime Student: INR 499/year (valid student ID required)
- Prime Family: INR 1,499/year (up to 4 family members)

1.2 Free Trial
- 30-day free trial available for new members
- No charge during trial period; cancel anytime
- Trial automatically converts to paid membership unless cancelled
- One free trial per customer (per email/phone number)

2. PRIME BENEFITS

2.1 Shipping Benefits
- FREE unlimited express shipping (2-3 day delivery) on all eligible orders
- FREE same-day delivery in metro cities for orders above INR 299
- FREE scheduled delivery with choice of time slot
- No minimum order value for free shipping
- Priority processing: Prime orders are processed first

2.2 Exclusive Deals & Discounts
- Early access to all sale events (30 minutes before general access)
- Prime-exclusive deals and lightning offers
- Additional 5% cashback on ShopEase-branded products
- Birthday month special: 10% off on one order (maximum discount INR 500)
- Anniversary month special: Extra 5% off on one order

2.3 Prime Video & Entertainment
- Access to ShopEase Prime Video streaming library
- Ad-free music streaming on ShopEase Music
- Free e-books and magazines from ShopEase Reading
- One free audiobook per month

2.4 Prime Customer Support
- Dedicated Prime support line (no wait time)
- Priority email response (within 4 hours)
- Extended return window: 45 days instead of 30 days
- Free return shipping on all returns

3. ELIGIBILITY & ENROLLMENT

3.1 Who Can Join
- Any ShopEase customer with a verified account
- Must be 18 years or older (or have parental consent)
- Student tier requires valid .edu email or student ID verification
- Family tier: primary member must be a Prime Annual subscriber

3.2 How to Enroll
Step 1: Go to shopease.com/prime or the ShopEase app
Step 2: Choose your preferred plan
Step 3: Enter payment details (auto-renewal enabled by default)
Step 4: Start enjoying Prime benefits immediately

3.3 Family Member Addition (Prime Family)
- Primary member can invite up to 3 additional family members
- Each family member gets their own separate account
- Shipping benefits shared; each member gets individual deal access
- Family members can be added/removed at any time

4. BILLING & CANCELLATION

4.1 Payment
- Auto-renewal: membership renews automatically at the end of each billing cycle
- Payment methods: credit/debit card, UPI, or ShopEase Wallet
- Billing date reminder sent 7 days before renewal

4.2 Cancellation Policy
- Cancel anytime from account settings
- Monthly plan: no refund for the current billing month
- Annual plan: prorated refund for unused months (minus 1 month processing fee)
- Benefits continue until end of current billing period after cancellation
- Cancelled members can re-join at any time

4.3 Membership Pause
- Pause membership for up to 3 months per year
- No charges during pause period
- Benefits suspended during pause
- Membership automatically resumes after pause period

5. TERMS & CONDITIONS

5.1 Fair Use Policy
- ShopEase reserves the right to limit or terminate memberships in cases of abuse
- Sharing membership credentials is not allowed
- Reselling Prime benefits is prohibited
- Maximum 10 Prime-eligible orders per day

5.2 Changes to Program
- ShopEase may modify membership benefits with 30 days' notice
- Price changes apply only at next renewal cycle
- Members will be notified via email and in-app notification

5.3 Dispute Resolution
- Membership disputes are handled by our Prime Support team
- Escalation path: Prime Support > Customer Relations Manager > Ombudsman
- Response within 48 hours for all escalated disputes

- Email: prime@shopease.com
