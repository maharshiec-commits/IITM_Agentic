ShopEase E-Commerce - Return & Refund Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Document ID: POL-RET-001

1. GENERAL RETURN POLICY

1.1 Return Window
Customers may return most items purchased from ShopEase within 30 days of delivery. The return window begins on the date the item is delivered to the shipping address, as confirmed by tracking information.

1.2 Condition Requirements
All returned items must be:
- In their original, unused condition
- In the original packaging with all tags attached
- Accompanied by the original receipt or order confirmation
- Free from any signs of wear, damage, or alteration

1.3 Non-Returnable Items
The following categories are NOT eligible for return:
- Perishable goods (food, flowers, plants)
- Personal care items (cosmetics, skincare opened/used)
- Intimate apparel and swimwear (for hygiene reasons)
- Customized or personalized products
- Digital downloads and software licenses
- Gift cards and prepaid cards
- Hazardous materials and flammable liquids
- Items marked as "Final Sale" or "Non-Returnable" on the product page

2. REFUND PROCESS

2.1 Refund Timeline
Once we receive and inspect your returned item:
- Refund approval/denial notification: within 3 business days of receipt
- Credit card refunds: 5-10 business days after approval
- Debit card refunds: 5-10 business days after approval
- Store credit: issued immediately upon approval
- UPI/Net Banking refunds: 3-7 business days after approval
- Cash on Delivery orders: refunded via bank transfer within 7-10 business days

2.2 Refund Amount
- Full purchase price is refunded for items returned in original condition
- Original shipping charges are NOT refunded unless the return is due to our error (wrong item, defective product)
- Return shipping costs are borne by the customer for change-of-mind returns
- If a promotional discount was applied, refund will be for the actual amount paid

2.3 Partial Refunds
Partial refunds may be issued for:
- Items returned after 30 days but within 45 days (subject to 15% restocking fee)
- Items with missing original packaging (10% deduction)
- Items showing minor signs of use (up to 25% deduction based on condition)

3. EXCHANGE POLICY

3.1 Direct Exchanges
- Size and color exchanges are available within 30 days
- Exchange items must be of equal or lesser value
- If exchange item costs more, customer pays the difference
- If exchange item costs less, the difference is issued as store credit

3.2 Exchange Shipping
- Free exchange shipping for size/color issues
- Customer pays shipping for preference-based exchanges

4. ELECTRONICS RETURN POLICY

4.1 Extended Inspection Period
Electronics have a 15-day return window (not 30 days).

4.2 Requirements
- All original accessories, cables, manuals, and packaging must be included
- Device must be factory reset before return
- Serial number must match the one on the original invoice
- Activated software licenses cannot be returned

4.3 Damaged Electronics
- If the product arrives damaged, report within 48 hours of delivery
- Include photographic evidence of damage
- A replacement or full refund will be processed within 2 business days

5. LARGE ITEMS & FURNITURE

5.1 Return Process for Large Items
- Large items (furniture, appliances over 30 kg) require scheduled pickup
- Schedule pickup through your account or by contacting customer support
- Pickup will be arranged within 3-5 business days
- Items must be disassembled and repackaged (if originally packaged for assembly)

5.2 Restocking Fee
- Large items returned for change-of-mind are subject to a 20% restocking fee
- No restocking fee for defective or damaged large items

6. DISCOUNTED & SALE ITEMS

6.1 Sale Items
- Items purchased during sales events follow the standard return policy
- Refund will be for the discounted price paid

6.2 Coupon/Promo Code Purchases
- If a coupon or promo code was used, the refund reflects the actual amount paid
- The coupon/promo code will NOT be reissued upon return
- Bundle deals: returning one item from a bundle may void the bundle discount

7. HOW TO INITIATE A RETURN

Step 1: Log into your ShopEase account
Step 2: Go to "My Orders" and select the order
Step 3: Click "Return/Exchange" next to the item
Step 4: Select return reason and preferred refund method
Step 5: Print the prepaid return label (if eligible) or arrange pickup
Step 6: Ship the item within 7 days of return authorization
