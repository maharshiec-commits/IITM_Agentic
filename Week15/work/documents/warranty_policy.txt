ShopEase E-Commerce - Product Warranty Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Document ID: POL-WRT-001

1. WARRANTY OVERVIEW

1.1 ShopEase Assurance Warranty
All products sold on ShopEase come with either:
- Manufacturer's warranty (duration and terms set by the manufacturer)
- ShopEase Assurance Warranty (for products without manufacturer warranty)

1.2 ShopEase Assurance Coverage
- Electronics: 1 year from date of purchase
- Appliances: 1 year from date of purchase
- Furniture: 6 months from date of delivery
- Clothing & Accessories: 30 days from date of delivery (manufacturing defects only)
- Footwear: 90 days from date of delivery (manufacturing defects only)

2. WHAT IS COVERED

2.1 Covered Under Warranty
- Manufacturing defects in materials or workmanship
- Functional failures under normal use conditions
- Missing parts that were supposed to be included
- Product not performing as described in specifications

2.2 NOT Covered Under Warranty
- Damage from misuse, abuse, or negligence
- Normal wear and tear
- Damage from unauthorized modifications or repairs
- Cosmetic damage (scratches, dents) not affecting functionality
- Damage from natural disasters, power surges, or Acts of God
- Products with tampered or removed serial numbers
- Consumable parts (batteries, filters, bulbs) after initial period
- Damage from using the product outside recommended conditions

3. WARRANTY CLAIM PROCESS

3.1 How to File a Claim
Step 1: Log into your ShopEase account
Step 2: Navigate to My Orders > select the product
Step 3: Click "Warranty Claim" and describe the issue
Step 4: Upload photos or videos showing the defect
Step 5: Submit the claim

3.2 Evaluation Timeline
- Claim acknowledgment: within 24 hours
- Initial evaluation: 3-5 business days
- Physical inspection (if required): 7-10 business days
- Resolution: within 15 business days of claim submission

3.3 Resolution Options
Depending on the issue, we may:
- Repair the product at no cost
- Replace the product with an identical or equivalent model
- Issue a full refund if repair or replacement is not possible
- Provide store credit for the purchase value

4. EXTENDED WARRANTY (ShopEase Protect+)

4.1 Plan Options
- Protect+ 1 Year: extends warranty by 1 additional year
- Protect+ 2 Year: extends warranty by 2 additional years
- Protect+ Complete: covers accidental damage for 2 years

4.2 Pricing
- Protect+ plans range from INR 199 to INR 4,999 based on product category and value
- Must be purchased within 15 days of product delivery
- Non-transferable and non-refundable

4.3 Accidental Damage Coverage (Protect+ Complete only)
- Covers drops, spills, screen cracks, and electrical surges
- Deductible of INR 500 per claim for items under INR 10,000
- Deductible of INR 1,000 per claim for items between INR 10,000 - 50,000
- Deductible of INR 2,500 per claim for items above INR 50,000
- Maximum 2 claims per year

5. WARRANTY REGISTRATION

5.1 Automatic Registration
- Products purchased on ShopEase are automatically registered for warranty
- No separate registration required
- Warranty is linked to your ShopEase account and order number

5.2 Proof of Purchase
- Your digital invoice/receipt serves as proof of purchase
- Keep your order confirmation email for reference
- Physical invoice included in the shipment can also be used

6. MANUFACTURER WARRANTY SUPPORT

6.1 Authorized Service Centers
- For manufacturer warranty claims, you may contact the manufacturer's authorized service center directly
- ShopEase can assist in connecting you with the manufacturer
- List of authorized service centers available on the product page

6.2 ShopEase Mediation
- If you face issues with manufacturer warranty claims, ShopEase will mediate on your behalf
- Contact our Warranty Support team with claim details
- Resolution provided within 10 business days
